# Problem: Move Zeroes (Easy)

**LeetCode Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/move-zeroes/

## Approach

Move all non-zero elements to the front while keeping their original order.

Fill the remaining positions with zeroes.

## Time Complexity

O(n)

## Space Complexity

O(1)
